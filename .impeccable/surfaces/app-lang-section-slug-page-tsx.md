and `public/landing-v2/piuudito/origin.json`. The case groups all three
sites under the same client. App/software and AI pages show the existing
localized Maestro/GymTree captures, explicitly labeled personal products;
AI also links to the personal `claude-local-docs` repository. The pages

the global reset. Reading, links and FAQ disclosure work without JavaScript.
The optional analytics panel gives refusal and consent equal treatment;
the preferences control remains at the bottom in normal flow.
